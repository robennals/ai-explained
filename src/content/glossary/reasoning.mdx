**Reasoning** is a model writing out its working before it answers, in tokens the app usually hides from you.

Nothing about the model changes to do this. The thinking is predicted one token at a time exactly like the answer, and the model reads it back as it goes, so its own output becomes its rough paper. A model trained to do this is called a *reasoning model*.

Questions where thinking first changes the answer:

- **A bat and a ball cost $1.10, and the bat costs $1.00 more than the ball** — answer immediately and you say 10 cents, like most people. Check it and you get 5 cents.
- **How many r's in "strawberry"** — the model has to spell the word out before it can count.
- **Scaling a recipe** — the rice doubles, but the cooking time does not, and spotting that takes a second thought.

Reasoning models came from training with answers a computer could check automatically, which meant good reasoning could be rewarded without anyone having to define what good reasoning looks like.
