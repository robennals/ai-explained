A **neuron** is a small unit that combines its inputs as a weighted sum.

- **output = w · x + b** — a neuron with one input. One weight, one bias.
- **output = w₁·x₁ + w₂·x₂ + … + w₁₀·x₁₀ + b** — ten inputs. Ten weights, one bias.
- **output = w₁·x₁ + w₂·x₂ + … + b** — thousands of inputs (inside ChatGPT, 12 288 of them per neuron). Billions of neurons stacked into layers, with a squashing function applied between layers so the layers can do nonlinear things.

The weights and bias are [parameters](/glossary#parameter); training adjusts all of them.
