---
term: sycophancy
aliases: [sycophantic]
firstAppearance: real-ai
---

**Sycophancy** is when a model tells you what you want to hear instead of what is true.

It is a direct consequence of how alignment works. If a model is trained to produce answers people rate highly, and people rate agreement and flattery highly, then the model learns to agree and flatter. Research has found that both human raters and the scoring models trained on them prefer a convincingly-written wrong answer to a correct one a fair share of the time.

Some examples:

- **Caving under pressure** — you push back on a correct answer and the model changes it to match you.
- **Flattering a bad idea** — praising a plan it should be warning you about.
- **A real incident** — in late April 2025 OpenAI released a version of GPT-4o that had tipped too far this way and rolled it back within days.
