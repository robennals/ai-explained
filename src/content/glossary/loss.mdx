A **loss** is a number that says how wrong a [model](/glossary#model)'s predictions are.

- **squared(predicted, actual) = (predicted − actual)²** — predict 5 when the truth is 3 → loss is 4. Predict 3 → loss is 0. Penalises big mistakes more than small ones.
- **absolute(predicted, actual) = |predicted − actual|** — same idea, gentler on big mistakes.
- **crossEntropy(probabilities, correctChoice) = −log(probability of the right answer)** — for classification. Model says 90% "cat" and the answer is "cat" → tiny loss. Says 1% "cat" → huge loss.

[Training](/glossary#training) is the process of making the loss as small as possible.
