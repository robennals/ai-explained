**IndexShare** is GLM-5.2's technique of reusing one indexer's shortlist of relevant words across several neighboring layers, instead of recomputing it at every layer.

Sparse attention still needs its indexer to scan every word in the context, and that scan itself is a real cost once the context reaches a million words. Neighboring layers tend to want nearly the same words: if a word matters to one layer, it usually matters to the next one too. So instead of running the indexer at every layer, GLM-5.2 runs it once every four layers and reuses that shortlist for the three layers after it.

Some examples:

- **A 100-layer model** — running the indexer at every layer means 100 scans; IndexShare cuts that to 25.
- **GLM-5.2's reported saving** — about 2.9 times fewer computations than recomputing the indexer at every layer, a big part of what makes its million-word context window practical.

IndexShare doesn't make any single scan cheaper; it just avoids paying for the same scan over and over across neighboring layers.
