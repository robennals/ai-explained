A **prefix** is the start of a piece of text, up to the point where the next word is missing.

A language model is given a prefix and has to predict the word that comes next. The blank at the end marks the missing word.

Some examples:

- **"I like to pet my ___"** — a prefix that is usually followed by "dog" or "cat".
- **"I was bitten by a ___"** — followed by "dog" far more often than "wolf", because people meet far more dogs than wolves.
- **"The capital of France is ___"** — a prefix where predicting the next word means knowing a fact.
- **A whole conversation so far** — when a chatbot writes a reply, everything said up to that point is the prefix, and it adds one word at a time.

The prefix a model is working from is also called its context.
