**Attention** is the mechanism that lets each token in a sequence choose which other tokens to focus on, instead of being stuck looking at a fixed window of nearby words.

Before attention, language models had to read a fixed number of recent words and predict from there. Attention solves this by giving each token three vectors — a query (what it's looking for), a key (what it offers), and a value (what it shares). Each token's query is matched against every other token's key by dot product; the highest-scoring matches contribute their values most strongly. The token's new representation is the weighted blend.

Some examples:

- **A pronoun resolving itself** — "she" looks back through the sentence with a query for "female noun"; the key on "the woman" matches; "she" pulls in the value to know who's being referred to.
- **Disambiguating "bank"** — in "she made a deposit at the bank", "bank" asks "is there a money word nearby?"; "deposit" answers yes; "bank" updates toward the financial meaning rather than the river one.
- **A pronoun resolved across paragraphs** — a novel introduces "John" once at the start; thousands of words later, "John picked up the scalpel" — attention can reach back to the original introduction. No fixed window can do this.
- **Inside ChatGPT** — every token in your prompt and the model's response uses attention to gather information from every previous token, in parallel, at every layer.

Attention is the single biggest reason modern AI works as well as it does — it's the *A* in *Attention Is All You Need*, the paper that introduced the transformer.
