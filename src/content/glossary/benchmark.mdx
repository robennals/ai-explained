A **benchmark** is a fixed set of test questions used to compare models against each other.

Benchmarks make progress measurable, but they wear out. Once models get close to full marks, a benchmark stops telling you which model is better, and the field moves to a harder one. That is why no single test covers the whole history of language models: each was useful for a few years and was then retired.

Some examples:

- **LAMBADA** — an early test of predicting a word that needs long-range context. Used for GPT-2 and GPT-3.
- **MMLU** — general knowledge across many subjects. The workhorse from about 2020 to 2024, until top models reached roughly 90% and it stopped separating them.
- **GPQA Diamond** — graduate-level science questions, hard enough to still discriminate.
- **SWE-bench Verified** — fixing real bugs in real software projects.
