**Post-training** is everything done to a model after pre-training, to teach it how to use what it already knows.

It does not add facts. It picks, out of everything the model could say, the kind of thing you actually wanted. It is a thin layer of training compared with pre-training, and it is the whole difference between a base model and something like ChatGPT.

Some ways it is done:

- **Show it good answers** — people write ideal responses by hand and the model is trained on them.
- **Let someone pick a winner** — a person sees two answers and says which is better, and the model is pushed towards the winner.
- **Let a model judge** — one copy of the model scores another's answer against written rules, which is far cheaper than paying people.
- **Check the answer** — for maths and code, the answer can be checked automatically, so no human or judge is needed at all.

Every one of these is a way of scoring answers, so that better answers become more likely next time.
