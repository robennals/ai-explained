**Reinforcement learning** is training by trial, error, and reward rather than by copying correct answers.

Most training in this tutorial works by showing a model the right answer and nudging it closer. Reinforcement learning works differently: the model attempts something, gets a score, and adjusts to earn higher scores in future. That matters when there is no single right answer to copy, only better and worse attempts. It is how models are taught to be helpful, and how reasoning models learn to think.

Some examples:

- **Learning from people** — the score comes from a model trained to predict which answers humans prefer.
- **Learning from a checker** — for maths and code the score comes from a program that verifies the answer, which cannot be flattered or fooled.
- **Learning to reason** — rewarding only correct final answers led one model to teach itself to work through problems step by step and check itself along the way.
