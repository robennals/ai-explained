A **base model** is a language model that has learned to predict the next word and has had no other training.

It knows an enormous amount, because predicting text well requires knowing how the world works. What it does not know is that your words are a request meant for it. Ask a base model a question and it continues the document your question probably came from.

Some things a base model does when you ask it a question:

- **Asks more questions** — "How do I stop my sourdough starter going mouldy?" gets three more baking questions, because that is what an FAQ page looks like.
- **Writes your side of the conversation too** — it answers, then makes up your next message and answers that one as well.
- **Does not stop** — it gives the right answer and then rolls on into "Share this article" and the next headline.
- **Copies whoever wrote the page** — it can continue as a confident stranger on a forum, right or wrong.

Turning a base model into something that answers you is what post-training is for.
