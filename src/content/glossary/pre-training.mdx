**Pre-training** is the first and much larger stage of training a language model, where it reads an enormous amount of text and learns to predict the next word.

Nothing in pre-training tells the model to be helpful. It is only ever asked what comes next. Getting good at that means learning grammar, facts, arithmetic, and how people behave, because all of those are needed to guess the next word correctly.

What pre-training gives a model:

- **Spelling and grammar** — after enough text, "the cat sat on the" is followed by "mat", not "mat's".
- **Facts about the world** — it has seen that Everest is 8,849 m often enough to predict it.
- **How people write** — a recipe, a legal contract, and a forum argument each continue differently.
- **Reasoning it was never taught** — to predict the last word of a murder mystery, it helps to have worked out who did it.

Pre-training uses almost all of the compute that goes into a model, often more than 99%.
