A **vector** is a list of numbers.

- **[3, 1]** — a 2D point: 3 east, 1 north.
- **[255, 128, 0]** — a colour: red 255, green 128, blue 0 (so, orange).
- **[2, 5, 1, 3]** — a grocery list by quantity: 2 apples, 5 oranges, 1 bread, 3 milks.
- **[0.21, −0.43, 0.07, …, 0.55]** (300 numbers) — a word's embedding in a small model.
- A vector of 12 288 numbers — one token's representation inside GPT-3.

What makes vectors useful isn't just storing numbers — you can compare them, add them, and measure distances between them, all with simple arithmetic.
