A **chat model** is a base model that has been through post-training, so that it answers you instead of continuing whatever document your words came from.

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are all chat models. Almost everything they know about the world they knew as base models, because that is where the knowledge came from. What post-training added is the habit of treating your message as a request meant for them.

What that changes:

- **It answers** — ask it how to stop a sourdough starter going mouldy and you get advice, rather than three more baking questions.
- **It takes turns** — it stops when its reply is finished, instead of carrying on and writing your next message too.
- **It knows whose words are whose** — the conversation reaches it as one stream of text with markers saying who is speaking, and it has been trained to continue only its own part.
- **It behaves consistently** — its tone, its formatting, and what it declines to do are all decided in post-training rather than by whoever happened to write the text it was pre-trained on.

The model underneath is the same machine doing the same thing: predicting one token at a time.
